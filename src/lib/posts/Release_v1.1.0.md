## Find anything, filter everything

Version 1.1.0 is all about getting you to the right content faster. Two big pieces:
a search bar that actually searches the whole site, and a ground-up rebuild of
browse filtering — including the long-requested ability to filter by more than one
tag at a time.

## A search bar that searches everything

The search box at the top of every page used to just drop you into the maps browse.
Now it's a proper site-wide search.

- **Type-ahead suggestions.** As you type, a dropdown surfaces matching maps, modes,
  playlists, and creators right away — click one to jump straight to it.
- **A real results page.** Press Enter (or pick "Search for…") and you land on a
  dedicated search page that shows everything matching your query, grouped into
  sections: Creators, Maps, Modes, Prefabs, and Playlists. Each section links
  through to the full list.

Whether you half-remember a map name or want to find a specific creator, the search
bar now takes you there in one step.

## Rebuilt browse filtering

The filter bar on the browse pages was doing too much in one cramped row, so we
rebuilt it from the ground up.

- **A slim, focused bar.** The bar now keeps just the essentials — type, search,
  sort, and a single **Filters** button — so it stays clean at any screen size.
- **One Filters panel.** Click **Filters** to open a tidy panel (a dropdown on
  desktop, a slide-up sheet on mobile) holding everything: tags, contributor,
  hide 343 content, and more — with room to breathe.
- **Applied filters at a glance.** Whatever you've filtered by shows up as removable
  chips right under the bar. See exactly what's active and clear any of it with a
  single tap.

## Filter by multiple tags

The headline filtering feature: **multi-tag filtering**.

- **Add as many tags as you need.** Stack up to ten tags and the results narrow to
  only the assets that have *all* of them — so "slayer" plus "btb" gets you exactly
  the big-team slayer maps.
- **Tag autocomplete.** Start typing and Unggoy suggests real tags from across the
  community, with usage counts, so you don't have to guess the exact wording.
