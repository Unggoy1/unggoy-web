## Unggoy is 1.0

After a long string of 0.9.x releases, Unggoy is officially version 1.0.0. The headline feature of this milestone is one that's been a long time coming: **creator profile pages**. Every gamertag across the site now leads somewhere meaningful.

## Creator profiles

Click any creator's gamertag — on a map, a mode, a playlist, or your own card — and you'll land on their profile at `/creator/<gamertag>`. It's built to feel like a Halo service record, not just another browse page.

- **A service-record header** shows the creator's emblem, gamertag, service tag, and a "creator since" year, alongside their headline stats: total plays, owned maps, modes, prefabs, average rating, and total bookmarks.
- **A "Most Played" row** highlights their top creations right up top, so you get an instant sense of their best work.
- **Browsable content tabs** for Maps, Modes, Prefabs, and Playlists. Each tab has the same Sort, order, and Per-page controls you already know from Browse, and the results live in their own panel just like the rest of the site.
- **An Owner / Contributor toggle** lets you switch between the assets a creator owns and the ones they only contributed to — so collaborative work shows up without muddying their own portfolio counts.
- **Share-friendly** — every profile has a "Copy profile link" button, and the page is fully responsive down to mobile.

## Getting there

- Creator links across the site — map and mode contributors, the playlist author, and your own profile card in the mobile menu — now open the new profile page.
- The account dropdown in the top corner gained a **My Profile** shortcut, right under "Create New Playlist," for jumping straight to your own page.

## Under the hood

Reaching 1.0 also meant making the site easier to find. Profiles, maps, modes, prefabs, and playlists are now published in a sitemap with proper structured data, so search engines can discover and index community content — and a creator's profile is a single link they can share anywhere.

As always, thank you for using Unggoy and for all the feedback that got us to 1.0. There's plenty more planned — if you have an idea or want to contribute, don't hesitate to reach out.
